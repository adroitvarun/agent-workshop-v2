# Student Handbook (sample)

## Attendance
Students need at least 75% attendance in each course to sit its end-semester exam. Students between 65% and 75% may apply to the Dean for condonation with a medical certificate.

## Grading
Grades run from O (10 points) to F (0 points). A course is passed with a grade of P (4 points) or higher.

## Hostel
Hostel gates close at 22:30. Late entry needs a signed pass from the warden. Cooking appliances are not allowed in rooms.

## Library
Students can borrow up to 4 books for 14 days. The late fee is Rs 5 per day, per book.
